How to use
  • Set any metronome, a physical one or an app, to the tempo shown. If it can accent beats, let it accent the one you count.
  • Start the exposure on the count of zero. Keep counting, and cover the next patch of the strip at each count in the table.
  • Ticking "Count each step from zero" restarts the count for every patch. This can be easier to count when you want to stop exposing between each patch.
  • If your metronome lacks the tempo shown, press "My metronome can't do …". The next best tempo appears, and the choice is remembered on this device.
  • There is purposely no built-in metronome. If you want that, it's better to simply find an f-stop timer, something like GoTimer's enlarger timer. This website is specifically for using a dedicated metronome, and the accuracy it provides.
  • The strips approximate Ilford Multigrade IV RC Deluxe at the filter set above the table, in the zones of the Zone System: 0 is full black, V middle grey and X paper white. Read them up and down. A place along the strips is one part of the picture: find its tone in the reference row, and the zone straight above or below is what that part prints as at that row's exposure. The reference row is the boxed one. It starts at the base: tap or click another row to make that one the reference. Your prints may differ.
  • Slide a finger sideways across a row, or drag with the mouse, to follow one part of the picture: every strip then shows its tone in full, and the zone it is nearest to. The row you slide on becomes the reference.
  • Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
